    A letter from us

    Born from love,
    built for families.

    Sanjeevani was born from a deeply personal experience — the loss of a loved one to a preventable health condition. That moment sparked a mission: to ensure no family has to go through the same pain.

    We believe that with the right technology, every family in India can access preventive healthcare that's personalized, culturally relevant, and emotionally intelligent. Sanjeevani isn't just an app — it's a companion that genuinely cares about your well-being.
